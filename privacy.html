Privacy Policy

Bachata Library · Last updated 19 September 2026

Bachata Library is a personal web app for organizing and watching dance-lesson recap videos. It has no server. Everything runs in your browser and talks directly to Google Drive on your behalf.

What the app accesses

  • Google Drive, limited scope. The app requests the drive.file permission, which only lets it see and manage files and folders it created itself. It cannot read the rest of your Drive.
  • Your email address. Used only to remember which Google account to sign in with next time. It is stored in your browser, nowhere else.

Where your data lives

Your videos, thumbnails, and the index of lessons, schools, figures, and notes are stored in a folder named Bachata Library in your own Google Drive. The app keeps a copy of the index and a short-lived sign-in token in your browser's local storage so the library opens quickly. Delete the app or sign out and that local copy is removed.

What is never done

  • No data is sent to any server other than Google's APIs.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party scripts.
  • Nothing is shared with anyone. Your files stay private in your Drive.

Revoking access

Sign out inside the app, or remove Bachata Library from your Google account's third-party access page. Your files remain in your Drive either way.

Contact

The app is open source at github.com/JonathanTGold/bachata-library. Questions can be raised there.
