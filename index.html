What you're looking at

A static mockup with fake data. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored. The player uses a public sample clip so the controls can be tried for real.

  1. Library is the home screen. Chips filter by school, by private teacher, or untagged.
  2. Tap a card to open the Lesson. Try Mirror, 0.5x, and Loop A–B: tap once to set A, again to set B.
  3. The + button is where classification happens. Date comes from the video. Pick Group and choose the school, or pick Private and choose the teacher. Then type the figures.
  4. Untagged videos get a dashed card so nothing gets lost.

Resize the window narrower than 900px to see it as a full-screen phone app.
